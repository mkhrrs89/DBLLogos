How the letter grades are calculated

Each class receives a fixed-scale 0–100 strength score: 42% average potential of its top 5 prospects, 33% average potential of its top 20, 15% median potential, and 10% average overall rating of its top 5. Letter grades now use tighter, fixed bands to better distinguish classes with similar BBGM ratings: 64+ is A+, 62.5–63.9 A, 61–62.4 A−, 59.5–60.9 B+, 58–59.4 B, 56.5–57.9 B−, 55–56.4 C+, and lower scores continue in 1.5-point steps through C, D, and F. No grade distribution is forced. A class with missing potential data is marked N/A, not F. Grades based on fewer than five complete prospect ratings are provisional. This is an editorial scouting estimate, not an official Basketball GM grade or a prediction of career outcomes; ratings can change in future exports.
